KNOW THE FACTS

Don't believe every myth.

Correct information helps people make safe and confident decisions.

MYTH

Blood donation always makes you weak.

FACT

Many healthy eligible donors return to normal activities soon. Follow aftercare advice.

MYTH

Only rare blood groups can help.

FACT

Every blood group is important. Hospitals need different blood groups.

MYTH

Donation takes the whole day.

FACT

The visit includes registration, screening, donation and rest. Ask the centre about timing.

MYTH

You can donate even when unwell.

FACT

Your health comes first. Medical staff decide if donation is safe.
