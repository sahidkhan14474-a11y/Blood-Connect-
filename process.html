STEP BY STEP

How blood donation works.

Here are common steps at a blood donation centre.

01

Registration

You provide your basic details and complete the donor form.

02

Health Check

Trained staff ask health questions and perform required checks.

03

Donation

If eligible, trained professionals collect the donation using sterile equipment.

04

Rest and Refreshment

Take a short rest and follow the centre's aftercare instructions.

Every step is important.

Never skip health screening. Safe donation starts with informed decisions.
